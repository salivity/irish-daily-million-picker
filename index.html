Frequently Asked Questions

Comprehensive guide to Irish Daily Million lottery mathematics, odds, and combination betting.

What is the Irish Daily Million lottery format?

The Irish Daily Million is a numbers lottery where 6 main numbers plus 1 bonus ball are drawn from a single pool of 39 balls. Draws take place twice daily at 2:00 PM and 9:00 PM GMT, offering opportunities for both standard jackpot play and fixed-odds bookmaker wagers.

How do bookmakers offer bets on 1 to 5 numbers?

Leading high-street and online bookmakers allow punters to bet on matching exactly 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 numbers. Unlike official parimutuel tickets requiring 6 matches for the jackpot, straight line bets pay out fixed multiples as long as all your selected balls are drawn among the 6 (or 7) drawn balls.

What is the actual mathematical chance of matching 1 number?

In a 6-ball draw from 39 numbers, the exact probability of matching a single chosen number is 6/39, which equals 1 in 6.5 (15.38%). If betting in a market that includes the 7th bonus ball, the probability increases to 7/39, or exactly 1 in 5.57 (17.95%).

What is the exact probability of hitting a 2-number Double?

For 2 chosen numbers in a 6-ball draw, the formula simplifies to (6×5)/(39×38) = 30/1482, which is 1 in 49.4 (2.02%). In a 7-ball draw including the bonus, the chance improves to (7×6)/(39×38) = 42/1482, giving 1 in 35.29 (2.83%).

What is the true chance of hitting a 3-number Treble?

The true mathematical odds of matching a 3-ball treble from the 6 main numbers are (6×5×4)/(39×38×37) = 120/54834, or 1 in 456.95 (0.219%). In a 7-ball draw including bonus ball, the chance is (7×6×5)/54834 = 210/54834, or 1 in 261.11 (0.383%).

How does the Combination Wheel system work?

A lottery wheel takes a larger pool of chosen candidate numbers (such as 6 to 12 balls) and algorithmically produces every possible distinct combination of size 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5. This guarantees complete mathematical coverage across all your selected numbers.

How does the Results Checker work with drawn numbers?

The Results Checker compares your entered winning balls (or a simulated draw) directly against every generated line and wheeled combination. It automatically highlights matching balls in real time, marks winning slips, and tallies total winners.

How does the JSON state import and export work?

The JSON state system serializes all application preferences, including your active ball filters, custom named lists, generated lines, winning numbers, and saved ticket history. You can backup your setup and restore it on any device seamlessly.

Does this tool require an internet connection or aggregated data?

No. This tool operates entirely offline with zero real-time web scraping or third-party data aggregation. All combinatorial algorithms, odds calculations, sound synthesizers, and state persistence execute locally inside your web browser.
